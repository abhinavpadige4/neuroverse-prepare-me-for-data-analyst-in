## Key Concepts
- **Mean/Median/Mode**: Measures of central tendency
- **Standard Deviation**: Measure of spread
- **Null Hypothesis (H0)**: No effect/difference
- **Alternative Hypothesis (H1)**: There is an effect
- **P-value**: Probability of observing results if H0 is true
- **Significance Level (alpha)**: Threshold for rejecting H0
- **Lift**: Relative improvement in conversion rate
- **Funnel Analysis**: Tracking users through conversion steps
